# Planning Assumptions

| Assumption | Default / rule | Owner | Review cadence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Planning horizon | Rolling 12 months, monthly forecasts; weekly operational view | Finance Planning | Monthly |
| Capacity standard | Productive hours, not paid hours, is the capacity unit | Operations | Quarterly |
| Attendance factor | Scenario input by approved organization; baseline uses last certified trend | Operations / HRBP | Monthly |
| Hiring lead time | Job-family median time-to-fill and planned start lag | Talent Acquisition | Monthly |
| Attrition treatment | Baseline is trailing 12-month voluntary attrition, adjusted only with documented scenario rationale | HRBP | Monthly |
| Productivity baseline | Latest certified comparable period, quality-adjusted where available | Operations Analytics | Monthly |
| Currency and cost | Finance-approved currency, actual/forecast labeling, and allocation policy | Finance Planning | Monthly |
| Organization mapping | Effective-dated hierarchy, frozen for each certified close | People Operations | Monthly |
| Privacy threshold | Suppress employee-derived groupings below 10 people unless Privacy approves an exception | Legal / Privacy | Quarterly |

Scenario changes do not change historical actuals. Each published scenario must record its author, date, source period, and assumptions version.
