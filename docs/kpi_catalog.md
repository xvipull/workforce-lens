# KPI Catalog

| KPI | Definition / formula | Grain | Owner | Caveat |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Active headcount | Distinct active workers at period end | Org × period | People Operations | Worker count; not capacity. |
| FTE | Sum of active worker FTE fractions at period end | Org × period | People Operations | Contracted FTE, not productive hours. |
| Vacancy rate | Open approved positions ÷ approved positions | Org × period | Talent Acquisition | Requires approved-position denominator. |
| Voluntary attrition rate | Voluntary exits during period ÷ average active headcount | Org × period | HRBP | Interpret only with sufficient population and trend context. |
| Capacity gap | Required productive hours − available productive hours | Org × period | Operations | Does not prescribe a staffing action. |
| Available productive hours | Scheduled hours × attendance factor × productivity availability factor | Org × period | Operations | Factors are approved planning assumptions. |
| Labor cost per FTE | Labor cost ÷ average FTE | Org × period | Finance Planning | Excludes/labels any non-labor allocations. |
| Productivity | Accepted output units ÷ productive hours | Org × period | Operations Analytics | Compare only where output definition and quality gate match. |
| Quality-adjusted productivity | Accepted output units meeting quality threshold ÷ productive hours | Org × period | Operations Analytics | Quality threshold must be versioned. |
| Time to fill | Median days from approved requisition to accepted offer/start, per policy | Job family × period | Talent Acquisition | State milestone used in every report. |

All rates use documented denominators, and all planning scenarios must identify their assumption version.
